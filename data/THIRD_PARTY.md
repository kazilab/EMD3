# Third-party attribution, and why no dataset is bundled

**This deposit redistributes no third-party experimental data.** Every number in
`outputs/` is generated by the bundled code from chosen parameter values. There
is no `fetch_data.py` here, and no analysis step requires a download.

That is not an omission deferred for later. It is the finding that determines
what the EMD3 build is for, and it is recorded in full in
`code/emd3_simulation/fit/PROVENANCE.md`.

## The gap

The published exposure studies report **endpoint marker fractions**. The
question EMD3 poses — is a change in the stem-like fraction induction or
selection? — requires **longitudinal composition with absolute counts**. Those
are different measurements, and the second does not exist publicly for any
arsenic–stemness exposure series we could locate. Rather than dress a fit up out
of endpoint data, the deliverable was reframed to identifiability and
experimental design. The analysis then quantifies exactly how much difference
the missing measurement class makes.

These are **scholarly citations supporting structural choices**, not
redistributed material. No figure was digitised, no table was transcribed, and
no file from any of these sources is present in this deposit. `SOURCES.tsv`
records them with their identifiers and their acquisition status.

## Not acquired, and why

- **Transcompp's deposited datasets.** They would let the plasticity machinery
  be calibrated on real sorted-fraction series, which is the single most
  valuable improvement available to this build. Not attempted here; the baseline
  rates remain chosen. This is the recommended next step.
- **Any arsenic longitudinal composition series with absolute counts.** We could
  not locate one. If none exists, that is itself the argument for the designed
  experiment this build outputs.
- **Cadmium and the primary-cell comparator as held-out arms.** With endpoint
  fractions only they would add a second and third *unconstrained* parameter set
  rather than a test. Scoping them out is deliberate and stated rather than left
  as a silent gap.

## One caution about the exemplar

MCF-10A is a **spontaneously immortalised** line. The Boolean layer's
telomerase-negative start state is therefore not a validated representation of
it, and the immortalisation result is reported from both start states for that
reason. See check V17 and `fit/FINDINGS.md`.
